ABOUT DIYOFX

Made for editors.

DiyoFX is a small creative platform built for anime editors and Alight Motion creators.

01

What is DiyoFX?

DiyoFX is a place where I share my anime editing resources, including XML presets, editing materials and other assets created for my edits.

02

Built by an editor

DiyoFX was created from my own experience as an anime editor. The goal is simple — make useful resources available to other editors and help make the editing process easier.

03

What you can find here

You can find Alight Motion XML presets, editing materials, overlays, audio resources and other assets used in anime edits.

04

The goal

DiyoFX is built to grow with the editing community. More presets, materials and resources will be added over time.
